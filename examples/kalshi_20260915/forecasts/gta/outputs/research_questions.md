# Research questions before browsing

- Has the full game already released in the US on PS5, Xbox Series X/S or Windows? Has any announced date passed without an indication of delay?
- What is the latest official Rockstar release date and platform list? Does Take-Two corroborate or revise it?
- What are the latest observable production, certification, distribution, and marketing milestones? Distinguish announcements from completion.
- What previous announced dates were delayed, why, and how close to launch? What contrary current evidence indicates further delay?
- Is there any primary-source September 2026 update? If sources are stale or inaccessible, record the gap.
- Could the first platform release qualify despite other platforms being delayed? Could an announced-date clause qualify separately?
- Choose joint scenario timings and subjective weights only after research. No prediction-market prices or event-probability searches.
