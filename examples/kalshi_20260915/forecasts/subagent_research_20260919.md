Three subagents researched the existing Starship, GTA VI, and Federal Reserve contracts without receiving Kalshi prices or the prior forecast probabilities. Codex then audited the contract language and synthesized the results.

| Contract | Codex estimate | Earlier midpoint | Absolute difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Starship Flight 14 launch before Oct. 1 | 70% | 92% | 22 pp |
| GTA VI first full U.S. release by Nov. 30 | 93% | 90.5% | 2.5 pp |

The GTA VI estimate uses the actual contract wording: the event is the first U.S. release on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, **or** Windows, rather than requiring all three platforms. Rockstar's announced November 19 console release therefore dominates the estimate.

Sources used by the researchers include [SpaceX](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205,%202026.pdf), [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/space/stakeholder_engagement/spacex_starship), [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI), [Take-Two](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf), the [Federal Reserve September statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm), and the [FOMC calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm).
