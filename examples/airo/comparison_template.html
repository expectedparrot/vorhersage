04 / Reading the comparison
What the numbers establish
The workflow generated and imported a complete grid. Coherence checks test consistency; they do not measure forecasting accuracy.


One fresh model versus a four-model panel
The fresh model is Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview through EDSL. The authors used GPT-6 Astra, Fable 5.1, Opus 5, and GPT-5.5 Pro. Roster, date, and research differences prevent a controlled comparison of model or method quality.
Probability medians and paired multipliers
We compare the fresh probability with the authors’ unweighted median probability. For conditions, we divide each model’s conditional probability by its own same-session baseline, then take the authors’ median on the log scale. This is generally different from dividing two panel medians.
A zero denominator makes a multiplier undefined; “—” indicates an undefined value. Display rounding does not change the stored probabilities.
Reproduced source arithmetic
The offline reproduction matched all 376 checked published values. The authors’ reported unconditional audit found zero violations in 1,996 comparisons. The broader conditional audit found four inconsistencies in Opus 5.
Those audit scopes differ from the fresh model’s all-condition check below. The different-window “BRACKET” comparisons are retained in the authors’ diagnostic and excluded from Vorhersage’s logical implications.

A development pilot with limited research
Six EDSL continuations included an empty native-function-call failure and a truncated output. The controller made JSON-only delivery explicit, raised the output allowance, and recorded both failures and their costs. It preserved the incomplete text without repairing probabilities.
The model made ten searches, successfully read one page, and encountered one page-fetch failure. It omitted the requested recency filters and did not search again after reading. This limits fidelity to the authors’ research procedure.
Recorded protocol deviations
    The original instrument and ECI target remain fixed. The pilot’s forecast was finalized on September 12, 2026 (UTC). No original panel probabilities were deliberately supplied as model inputs; retrieved sources could still discuss other forecasts.
